04 / FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE

Curious is good.
Traceable is better.

Books give us ideas. Research helps us decide what those ideas can honestly claim. The numbers beside each scientific statement lead here.

Strong evidence

Consistent substantial research or established guidance for the stated claim.

Mixed evidence

Some support, with important limitations or disagreement.

Early evidence

Interesting, but too little reliable research to be confident.

These are editorial judgements, not formal clinical grades. Association is not causation. A full therapy is not a one-minute exercise. A starter time is not necessarily a tested health dose.

What we deliberately left out

No supplement stack, dopamine reset, rigid coffee delay, fasting window or elaborate morning routine is required by this guide. We did not establish enough added value to make them part of this simple programme. This is an editorial scope decision, not a claim that every use is ineffective.

Cold-water exposure has limited, varied evidence for general wellbeing and is not prescribed here.[19] Education should reduce unnecessary pressure, not create a new optimisation checklist.
