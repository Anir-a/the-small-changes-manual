# Publication rights and source use

Checked 25 October 2026. This is an editorial record, not legal advice or clearance.

## Purpose

One Small Shift is an independent educational comic about everyday wellbeing. Educational purpose describes intent. It does not create a blanket copyright exception, and it is not a medical treatment.

## Original writing and design

The explanations, fictional Alex story, speech bubbles, page layouts, diagrams and four-week plan were created for this project with AI assistance. Scientific references support the stated claims. Books are credited as influences, not treated as proof.

## What is not included

No book covers, scanned pages, publisher artwork, copied signature diagrams, substantial quotations, celebrity likenesses or third-party stock photos are included. The project does not imitate a named artist or claim endorsement by an author, publisher or medical organisation.

## Artwork provenance

Twenty-four fictional comic scenes were generated for this project. Their prompts use the project’s own character references and generic visual descriptions. The repository retains a provenance manifest. This records process; it does not guarantee that no third-party similarity or claim could arise.

## Limits of the review

Full source books were unavailable for complete comparison. No exhaustive plagiarism corpus, comprehensive image-similarity search, trademark search or jurisdiction-specific legal clearance was performed. No zero-risk copyright guarantee is offered.

## AI and ownership

The OpenAI terms address rights as between the user and provider, to the extent permitted by law. That is different from third-party clearance and from whether a jurisdiction grants copyright to AI-generated elements. No exclusive copyright is asserted over purely AI-generated elements.

## Rights and reuse

No broad open-source or Creative Commons licence has been granted for this project. To the extent copyright or other rights subsist and are held by the publisher, those rights are reserved, subject to applicable law and GitHub platform terms. References and dependency licences remain separate.

## Fonts and dependencies

The webpage uses system fonts. The PDF embeds DejaVu fonts; the applicable licence notice is retained with the source. Python libraries are build dependencies. Their source is not copied into this repository.

## Source and health review

Sources were checked 25 October 2026 through a targeted evidence review. Some scientific records were available as abstracts only. This is not a systematic review, a medical peer review or a complete preventive-health guide. Clinical advice must be individualised.

## Privacy

The optional tracker stores its marks and chosen action in browser local storage. Application code does not transmit this tracker data. Hosting providers may still process ordinary access logs under their own terms. Clearing browser storage removes the saved tracker.
